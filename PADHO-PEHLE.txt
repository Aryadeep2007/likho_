===========================================================
   LIKHO - apna blog, apne computer pe
   (pehle ye padh lo, 1 minute lagega)
===========================================================


KYA KARNA HAI (bas 3 step)
---------------------------------------------------------

1. Is ZIP file pe RIGHT-CLICK karo -> "Extract All..." ->
   "Extract" dabao.

   *** Ye step skip mat karna. ***
   Zip ke andar se seedha chalaoge toh kaam nahi karega.

2. Jo naya folder bana hai, usko kholo.

3. "run.bat" pe DOUBLE-CLICK karo.

Bas. Ek kaali window khulegi (wo normal hai, band mat karna)
aur 2-3 second me browser me tumhara blog khul jayega.


===========================================================


PEHLI BAAR CHALANE PE 2 POPUP AA SAKTE HAIN
---------------------------------------------------------

POPUP 1 - "Windows protected your PC" (neeli screen)

   Ye isliye aata hai kyunki humne Microsoft ko paise
   dekar apne app ko "sign" nahi karvaya. Har naye app
   ke saath hota hai.

   Karo ye:  "More info" pe click karo
             phir "Run anyway" pe click karo


POPUP 2 - "Windows Firewall" wala

   "Allow access" pe click karo.

   Ye sirf isliye chahiye taki tumhara PHONE bhi same
   wifi pe ye blog khol sake. Mana kar doge toh blog
   phir bhi chalega, bas phone wala feature band rahega.


===========================================================


ISME KYA KYA KAR SAKTE HO
---------------------------------------------------------

* "+ Naya post" se likhna shuru karo

* Ctrl+K dabao -> kuch bhi search karo

* Post ke andar [[kisi post ka naam]] likho, wo apne aap
  link ban jayega. Phir "Naksha" page pe jao - poora jaal
  dikhega ki kaunsa post kisse juda hai.

* Upar right me "QR" button hai. Click karo, phone se
  scan karo, blog phone me khul jayega.
  (phone aur computer same wifi pe hone chahiye)

* "Dashboard" pe dekho ki log tumhara post kahan tak
  padhte hain aur kahan bore hoke chhod dete hain.


===========================================================


TUMHARE POSTS KAHAN HAIN?
---------------------------------------------------------

Usi folder ke andar "blog-data" naam ka folder ban jayega.
Tumhare saare posts wahan simple text files me hain.

Matlab:
  - Notepad se bhi khol ke padh sakte ho
  - Pendrive me copy kar sakte ho
  - Kabhi ye app band bhi ho jaye toh bhi tumhara likha
    hua kahin nahi jayega

Poora backup ek file me chahiye? Neeche "Backup download"
pe click karo.


===========================================================


KUCH KAAM NAHI KAR RAHA?
---------------------------------------------------------

"Windows cannot find likho.exe"
   -> Zip extract nahi kiya hai. Step 1 dobara karo.

Kaali window khulti hai aur turant band ho jati hai
   -> Zip extract nahi kiya hai. Step 1 dobara karo.

"port 4000 already busy" likha aa raha hai
   -> Likho pehle se chal raha hai. Purani kaali window
      dhundo aur band karo.
   -> Ya doosre port pe chalao: kaali window me type karo
      run.bat 4001
      aur Enter dabao.

Browser khud nahi khula
   -> Koi baat nahi. Browser kholo aur address bar me
      type karo:  localhost:4000

Blog band kaise karein?
   -> Kaali window band kar do. Bas.
